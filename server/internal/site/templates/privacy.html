What the server software keeps

  • Your account: login name, display name, a hash of your password (never the password itself), when it was made and last used, your game data (level, currencies, items, settings, perk pages), your statistics and match history, your friends, recent players and blocked lists.
  • Logs of the connections: your internet address, your login name and what the game asked for (no password), kept as long as the operator sets (30 days by default).
  • For the cap on new accounts: how many accounts each internet address made today (no names).

You can download your data or delete your account on this site. The site sets one cookie, only to protect its forms; it has no tracking and no script.
